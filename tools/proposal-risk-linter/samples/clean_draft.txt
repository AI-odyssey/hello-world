Introduction

We understand that Client is seeking government relations support to navigate the evolving policy
environment in relation to the proposed amendment. Firm will support Client in identifying relevant
risks and opportunities and communicating its perspective effectively to key stakeholders.

Proposed Scope of Work

Firm will provide strategic and practical support for Client's engagement with mutually agreed
target stakeholders, subject to stakeholder availability and confirmation.
Firm will support the coordination of up to four external stakeholder meetings per quarter,
including preparation, attendance where strategically appropriate, and one round of follow-up
per meeting. Firm does not guarantee access to or meetings with any specific stakeholder.
Firm will conduct ongoing monitoring and intelligence gathering covering up to four agreed
priority areas, with one consolidated written update per month.

Any services outside the scope set out above will be subject to a separate written agreement
on scope, timing, and fees.

Professional Fees and Expenses

The monthly fee is based on an estimated involvement of 40 hours. Firm will notify Client when
approximately 80% of the monthly allocation has been used. Any work beyond the agreed allocation
will require prior written approval and will be charged at the applicable hourly rate.
